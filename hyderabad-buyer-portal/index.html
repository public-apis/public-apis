How we are paid

The incentives,
stated plainly.

We are a channel partner and we are paid by developers. That is precisely why we publish the full commission schedule, the objection list, and the projects we decline to represent.

₹0Charged to buyers — ever

No consultation fee, no access fee, no registration markup. If anyone asks you to pay us, it is not us.

2–4%Typical developer commission

Disclosed per project in writing before you book, including the tranche schedule and clawback terms.

41Builders tracked, not all sold

Some are profiled with warnings attached. We keep them in the directory because buyers deserve the record.

4hConsultant response window

Working hours, measured. If we miss it, the visit note is free of any follow-up pressure.

Commission transparency statement

Maison Hyderabad is operated by a residential channel-partnership practice serving end users in Hyderabad. We are compensated by the developer on successful closure, typically in tranches (booking, agreement and possession) per the developer's channel-partner agreement. This does not change the price you pay — builder-funded commissions are built into the developer's sales cost, not added to the buyer's invoice. We will hand you a written commission breakdown for any project you are considering, and we will tell you when a project pays us more than another we are recommending. Data on this site is illustrative demo content and not an offer or invitation to purchase.
